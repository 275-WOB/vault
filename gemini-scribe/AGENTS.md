# AGENTS.md

This file provides context about this Obsidian vault for AI agents.

## Vault Overview

This vault serves as an academic knowledge base and study repository for an undergraduate engineering degree, specifically focused on Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering. It contains lecture notes, problem sets, and revision materials spanning core disciplines such as aerodynamics, thermodynamics, materials science, electrical circuits, systems and control, and applied mathematics.

The vault is actively used to record modular coursework, practice problems, and exam revision materials structured around university curriculum modules.

## Organization

The vault follows a numbered top-level folder structure inspired by the Johnny.Decimal or PARA system, clearly separating intake, modular notes, coursework, and revision materials:

- `00 Inbox/`: Staging area for incoming or unfiled notes.
- `01 Modules/`: The primary knowledge repository, organized by university module codes and titles (e.g., `MAC111 Aerospace Design`, `MPS123 Maths`, `CMB128 Materials`, `ELE126 Electrical Fundamentals`). Notes within these subfolders correspond directly to specific lecture topics and engineering concepts.
- `02 Projects/` & `04 Resources/`: Dedicated spaces for project-based work and external reference material.
- `03 Revision/`: Subject-specific study aids, tutorial sheets, problem sets, and quizzes mapped to the core modules (e.g., `Thermofluids`, `Electrical Fundamentals`, `Aerospace Design`).
- `05 Templates/`: Houses reusable templates such as `Lecture-Template`.
- Support folders such as `_handwriting/`, `Ink/`, and `Attachments/` indicate integration with handwritten stylus notes, sketches, and diagrammatic annotations.

## Key Topics

- **Aerospace & Aerodynamics**: Generation of lift, drag, compressibility, flight mechanics, and air system lifecycle.
- **Statics & Thermofluids**: First law of thermodynamics (closed systems), energy conservation, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), and gas properties.
- **Materials Science**: Atomic bonding, stress analysis, ductility, strength, and hardness.
- **Engineering Mathematics**: Differentiation, complex numbers, matrices, functions, trigonometry, binomial expansion, and graph sketching.
- **Systems & Control**: Ordinary differential equations (ODEs) and mathematical modeling of dynamic systems.
- **Electrical Fundamentals**: Circuit analysis, RLC circuits, and network theorems.

## User Preferences

The user maintains a structured, academic note-taking approach focused on technical accuracy and conceptual clarity. Notes are divided logically by lecture topic, theory, and worked examples.

Responses should be technically precise, concise, and academically rigorous. Derivations, equations, and mathematical proofs should be clearly formatted using LaTeX notation (e.g., `$E = mc^2$`), and technical explanations should reflect university-level engineering depth without unnecessary filler.

## Custom Instructions

- When assisting with module notes, format mathematical equations, physical units, and thermodynamic/aerodynamic derivations using LaTeX syntax.
- Keep notes aligned with the modular structure (`01 Modules/[Module Code] [Module Name]/`) and use the conventions established in `05 Templates/Lecture-Template` where applicable.
- When creating or reviewing practice problems and worked examples, suggest placing them in `03 Revision/[Subject]/` rather than inside the main lecture notes.
- Support clear diagrammatic descriptions or ASCII/Mermaid diagrams where physical setups, free body diagrams, or circuit topologies are discussed.
